Gentle Dot · icon design

One shape everywhere: a glass violet orb with a bright core. The core is the assistant’s attention; the ring around it tells you what it is doing.

1 · The floating Dot on the desktop

It floats above every window and on every desktop, 56 × 56 pt. Drag it anywhere: when you let go, it snaps to the nearest edge so it never covers your work. Click it from anywhere to open the panel.
